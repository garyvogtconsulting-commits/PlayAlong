    Read from screenshots

    Add screenshots of a chord sheet, in any order. PlayAlong reads the chords and lyrics on your phone and fills in the song below for you to check.

    You can also drop or paste them here.

        Length is the song’s running time, like 3:45 from the recording. Intro is how long before the singing starts and Outro is how long the music plays after the last line. The sheet holds still for both and scrolls through the singing in between.

        How to write chords

        Use whichever style you have. Both can be transposed.

        Chords above lyrics, the way most chord websites show them. Keep each chord over the word it lands on:

        [Chorus]
G               D7
Come and sit by my side

        ChordPro, with each chord in square brackets right before the syllable:

        A[G]mazing grace, how [C]sweet the sound

        Guitar tab can go in as written. Six-line guitar tab is shown converted for baritone ukulele, and you can switch back to the original on the song:

        e|-----0-----|
B|---1---1---|
G|-0-------0-|
D|-----------|
A|-3---------|
E|-----------|

        A line on its own like [Chorus], Verse 2: or {c: Bridge} becomes a section heading. ChordPro tags like {title: …}, {key: …} and {duration: 3:45} fill in empty fields when you save.
